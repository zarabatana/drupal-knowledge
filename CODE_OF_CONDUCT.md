# Code of Conduct

Drupal Knowledge is a small project about getting Drupal facts right and
saying plainly what is not known. The way people treat each other here
should meet the same standard: accurate, direct and respectful.

## Expectations

- Be respectful. Disagree with claims, evidence and code, not with people.
- Be honest about uncertainty. "I don't know" and "I was wrong" are
  welcome sentences in this project; they are how the knowledge stays
  trustworthy.
- Be specific. A review comment names the source, the line or the test it
  rests on.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming intent.
- Respect the trust boundaries. Pressuring a maintainer to promote a claim
  to trusted knowledge without review is out of bounds, whoever asks.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion are not
tolerated, in the repository or in any space where the project is
represented.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline contributions and comments that
violate this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations. Decisions will be explained. To report a concern, contact the
maintainers through the private path in `SECURITY.md`; reports are handled
confidentially.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is original to the project and deliberately short. It
is compatible in spirit with the Drupal community's own code of conduct
(https://www.drupal.org/dcoc), which governs participation on Drupal.org.
